no ragrets

sep 22, 2026

sometimes, you might look back on your life and think about how you could have done things differently and feel immense regret. imagine you're on your deathbed, looking back on your whole life and thinking the same way. so many things could have been done differently. how you could have been bolder, taken more risks, and lived. how terrible. now come back to reality and realize that it doesn't have to be that way. the past cannot be changed; however, the future is unwritten. what you do now is in your hands. take action, be bold, do great things, have fun and live your best life. do not regret the future as well.

note:
1. i'm publishing this to commit myself to this principle and because it might be useful to others as well.
2. "ragrets" isn't a typo. it references a scene from a movie.
