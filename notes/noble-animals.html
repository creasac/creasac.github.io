noble animals

sep 22, 2026

some people look to animals and find that they're incapable of evil and suggest that to be an ideal for us to emulate. while animals do not commit evil, they are also incapable of good. some might say "well they do kill others". killing is necessary for some animals as part of their sustenance, without which they wouldn't survive and thus not even a matter of discussion in the first place. now some might argue that we as humans do not need to feed on meat since our dietary needs can be satisfied otherwise. while that's true, eating meat not only provides nourishment but also provides pleasure. indeed, neither humans nor animals would eat meat if not for the pleasure we derive through its consumption. at this stage of human evolution, pleasure is indeed an important human need. perhaps one day we'll evolve to find meat less appealing, which would be great. until then, any forceful attempt at depriving humans of such an important source of pleasure is itself not a good act.
